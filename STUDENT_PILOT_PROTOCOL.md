# EcoNova Campus Recycling Mobile MVP v1.0.0 — Student Pilot Protocol

## 1. Pilot Sample Sizing & Staged Rollout

### Phase 1: Micro-Pilot (Usability Gate)
* **Sample Size:** **5–10 Students**
* **Objective:** Identify major navigation roadblocks, screen confusion, or prompt issues before broader exposure.
* **Duration:** 1 Day
* **Gate Requirement:** All P1/P2 usability friction points resolved before proceeding to Phase 2.

### Phase 2: Campus Pilot (Validation Phase)
* **Sample Size:** **50–100 Students**
* **Target Audience:** Representation across Science, Engineering, Computer Science, Medicine, and Business faculties.
* **Duration:** 1 Week
* **Objective:** Gather statistical usability metrics (Success Rate, Task Time, NPS/Likert scores).

---

## 2. Tester Briefing & Minimal Guidance Protocol

### Briefing Script (Word-for-Word Instruction)
Facilitators must read the following statement to student testers at the start of each session:

> *"Welcome to the EcoNova pilot test. EcoNova is a campus mobile application designed to turn your recycling into Green Points, rewards, and inter-faculty competition. Today, we'd like you to complete a few simple tasks using the application. Please complete the tasks as naturally as you can. If something is confusing or hard to find, don't worry — you are testing the app, the app is not testing you! Please share your thoughts out loud as you tap through."*

### Facilitator Rules
1. **Do NOT teach:** Do not explain how screens work or where buttons are located prior to testing.
2. **Do NOT intervene prematurely:** Allow the student to explore naturally. Only provide a hint if the student is completely stuck for over 60 seconds.
3. **Record unobtrusively:** Log task completion time, wrong clicks, and direct quotes without judging the participant.

---

## 3. Privacy & Data Protection Safeguards

During the MVP Student Pilot:
* **No Unnecessary Personal Data:** Do NOT collect student national IDs, phone numbers, passwords, or home address information.
* **No Payment Credentials:** Do NOT request credit card, bank account, or mobile wallet details.
* **Anonymized Usability Data:** Feedback forms and task logs must be identified by participant codes (e.g. `P-001`, `P-002`) rather than personal identity.
* **Location Privacy:** Location data is simulated within campus boundaries (`Luxor University`) and strictly contained within the client application.
